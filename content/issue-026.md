# Short Stack #26 — Wed, Nov 18, 2026
*5 AI picks. Each one takes 10 minutes to try.*

---

### 1. GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol are out, and Astra got cheaper
OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra (frontier) and GPT-6.2 Sol (everyday) on Nov 17 in ChatGPT paid plans, Codex and the API. Astra costs $8 per million input tokens, $0.40 cached and $40 output, 20% below GPT-6 Astra; Sol keeps GPT-6.1 Sol's prices. OpenAI says Sol lands within 0.5 points of Astra on FrontierCode 2.0 at about a fifth of the cost (OpenAI's numbers).
**Try this in 10 min:** Swap your app's model to `gpt-6.2-sol`, rerun one real prompt, and compare output tokens and answer quality with what you use now.
Source: https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol/

### 2. MAI Code 2 lands in GitHub Copilot, with a Flash model on Free
Microsoft AI shipped two coding models on Nov 17. MAI Code 2 is for debugging and multi-file work; Flash is for quick edits. Microsoft says Code 2 gets 98% of GPT-6.1 Sol's score across four coding benchmarks at 40% of the cost (Microsoft's numbers). GitHub lists Flash for every Copilot plan including Free; Code 2 needs Pro or above.
**Try this in 10 min:** In VS Code, open Copilot Chat, pick MAI Code 2 Flash in the model picker, and hand it one small fix from your backlog. Compare the diff with your usual model.
Source: https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-17-mai-code-2-and-mai-code-2-flash-in-github-copilot/
Image: /issues/issue-026/pick-2.svg | Illustration: a code window with a small lightning bolt and a wrench beside it

### 3. Google AI Studio now builds full-stack Android apps
Since Nov 17, the AI Studio Build agent can turn one prompt into a Kotlin Android app with a Cloud Firestore database, Google Sign-In and Gemini calls that keep your API key out of the APK. New too: a faster HD emulator, one-click screenshots to the agent for UI fixes, and publishing to your personal Google Play library.
**Try this in 10 min:** Open `aistudio.google.com/apps`, select the Android chip, and describe a small app that needs sign-in and saved data. Fix one layout issue by sending an emulator screenshot to the agent.
Source: https://developers.googleblog.com/build-full-stack-android-apps-with-gemini-in-google-ai-studio/
Image: /issues/issue-026/pick-3.svg | Illustration: a phone with a small database cylinder and a key floating beside it

### 4. MAI Image 2.7 does precise edits and transparent backgrounds
Microsoft AI also released MAI Image 2.7 and a cheaper Flash version on Nov 17. Both change one object's color, material or position without disturbing the rest of the image, and output transparent backgrounds for logos, icons and product shots. In Microsoft Foundry preview, Microsoft lists about $35 per 1,000 images for 2.7 and about $10 for Flash.
**Try this in 10 min:** Sign in at MAI Labs (`microsoft.ai/models/mai-image-2-7`), upload a product photo, ask for one change, then ask for it on a transparent background.
Source: https://microsoft.ai/news/introducing-mai-image-2-7/
Image: /issues/issue-026/pick-4.svg | Illustration: a coffee mug on a checkerboard background with a paintbrush changing its color

### 5. Replit Missions: give agents a goal, a budget and rules
Replit launched Missions on Nov 17. You set an outcome, success criteria, a spending cap and which actions need approval; a swarm of agents builds and iterates while an AI judge checks the results. If the budget runs out first, the mission stops unresolved. Replit says a mission built DataCat, its own monitoring platform, in two days on a $3,000 budget, and open-sourced a self-hostable version.
**Try this in 10 min:** On `replit.com/missions`, set up one goal you've been putting off with a small cap and approval required before publishing. Check its success criteria before it starts.
Source: https://replit.com/blog/introducing-replit-missions
