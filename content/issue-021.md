# Short Stack #21 — Wed, Nov 11, 2026
*5 AI picks. Each one takes 10 minutes to try.*

### 1. Gemini 4 Carbon is out, at Argon's $2 / $10 price for now
Google made Gemini 4 Carbon available on Nov 10 in the Gemini API (paid tier), AI Studio and Gemini for business. It's Google's strongest model, built for long agent runs, hard multi-step coding and research. The intro price matches Gemini 4 Argon, $2 / $10 per million input/output tokens, then rises to $4 / $20 after Nov 30. Google says Carbon ranks #1 on CursorBench 4.0 and uses more tokens than Argon, which it still recommends for most work.
**Try this in 10 min:** Run one gnarly debugging prompt on Carbon and Argon side by side in AI Studio, and compare the answer and the token count before the price doubles.
Source: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/

### 2. Cursor Projects is now multiplayer
Cursor's Projects, where one coordinator directs a fleet of agents, now lets you invite teammates. Everyone keeps their own chat, but the Project shares requirement docs, specs, mockups and memory, plus a task board showing who (and which agent) is on what and the PR behind each task. Cursor says teammates' agents can also talk to each other to plan around overlapping work. It's in beta for all users as of Nov 10.
**Try this in 10 min:** Create a Project from Cursor's left nav, drop in one spec doc, invite a teammate, and give each of you one task on the board.
Source: https://cursor.com/blog/projects-multiplayer
Image: /issues/issue-021/pick-2.svg | Illustration: two cursors and three small robot heads gathered around one shared task board

### 3. Adobe MAX: Creative Agent spreads across Creative Cloud
At Adobe MAX on Nov 10, Adobe put its Creative Agent (an AI assistant that runs multi-step jobs) into Premiere, Illustrator, InDesign, After Effects and Frame.io, in beta. It also launched Firefly Design (beta), one canvas for raster, vector, motion, 3D and print, plus Relight in Photoshop. Local MCP access so outside agents can drive Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere and After Effects comes in private beta in early 2027.
**Try this in 10 min:** Open a short sequence in Premiere's AI Assistant (beta) and ask it to match color and exposure across every clip, then check what it changed.
Source: https://news.adobe.com/news/2026/11/adobe-transforms-creative-cloud-tools

### 4. You can now claim your business inside ChatGPT
OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta on Nov 10. In Business Hub, a verified business can update its details, add product feeds with pricing, and see aggregated mentions, website clicks and "discovery topics" where its site is cited. Eligible US ecommerce and online service businesses can also build a Business Agent that shoppers can chat with. OpenAI says it doesn't share users' chats with businesses.
**Try this in 10 min:** Ask ChatGPT what it knows about your company, then claim your listing in Business Hub and fix anything it got wrong.
Source: https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt
Image: /issues/issue-021/pick-4.svg | Illustration: a storefront awning inside a chat bubble with a check-mark badge

### 5. Runpod lets your coding agent run the GPUs
Runpod expanded Runpod for Agents on Nov 10: a hosted MCP server plus a library of tested skills, with plugins for Claude Code, Codex and ChatGPT. Agents can pick hardware, deploy Pods or Serverless endpoints, debug failures and shut things down. Every tool is tagged read-only or destructive, so clients can ask before deletions. Runpod says agents already issue over half of all CLI commands on its platform.
**Try this in 10 min:** Point your coding agent at agents.runpod.io, approve the connection, and ask it for a cost audit of what you're running right now.
Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/runpod-for-agents-lets-developers-hand-infrastructure-work-to-their-coding-agents-302934084.html
